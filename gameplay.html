Design direction

Everything connects.

Quests should introduce regions. Regions should create demand for professions. Professions should feed the economy. The economy should help towns grow. Events should bring those towns together. v1.1 now gives each of those systems a dedicated place on the website.

Server systems

The gameplay foundation behind the realm.

These are the major systems carried forward in the Woodland Realms server vision. Exact plugins and configurations can evolve during development, but the player experience remains centered on connected RPG progression.

MMO progression

Class-style abilities and character growth are designed around systems historically built with MMOCore and mcMMO, giving combat, gathering, and long-term skill development a reason to matter.

ClassesAbilitiesSkillsLevels

Towny roleplay

Players can build, trade, cooperate, and establish communities through a settlement-focused experience inspired by Towny gameplay. Towns are meant to become part of the realm's living history.

TownsTradeCommunityRoleplay

Jobs & economy

Professions such as farming, smithing, gathering, crafting, and treasure hunting create ways to earn currency and contribute useful goods to the wider player economy.

JobsGoldMarketsCrafting

Mythic encounters

Custom encounters developed with systems such as MythicMobs can turn roads, ruins, forests, and quest locations into dangerous spaces filled with unique enemies and event creatures.

Custom items & recipes

Foods, weapons, tools, materials, collectibles, and recipes are designed specifically for the realm, with custom textures reinforcing the medieval woodland atmosphere.

Hub activities

Side activities and minigames—including arcade-style experiences and competitive modes—can give players a change of pace between major RPG adventures.

Player cosmetics

Cosmetics, titles, achievements, and other visual rewards let players personalize their identity without replacing the progression and exploration at the center of the server.

Exploration first

Hidden roads, ruins, puzzles, treasure trails, settlements, and distant regions are meant to reward curiosity. Traveling through the world is part of the gameplay rather than empty space between objectives.

Custom resource experience

The project uses custom textures to give blocks, items, interfaces, and environmental details a more cohesive medieval-fantasy presentation.
